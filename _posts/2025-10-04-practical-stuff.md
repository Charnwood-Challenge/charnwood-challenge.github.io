So, here goes with some practical info. 

Please park at either Nanpantan sports ground or respectfully in residential areas, not outside the hut on the main road as it gets too busy. 

Please arrive in plenty of time to collect your number / register & of course join the last minute toilet queue. 

Fun run starts at 10.15 but arrive for 9.30 to register. 

There is a bag drop area. 

After the event there are hot drinks, cobs & cakes, so you may want to bring pennies / or your card too. 

Finally… just look at our beautiful new scout hut! This event helped make this possible & it will continue to put every penny we make to support our local children in Scouting. Thank you for all your support both this year & in previous years - & see you tomorrow!
